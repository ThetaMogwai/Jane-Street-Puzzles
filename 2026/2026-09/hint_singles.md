# Hint Singles

### Understanding the puzzle mechanism
Looking at the title of the album and the labels at the bottom reveals the core transformation of the puzzle : a **single letter deletion** *(or insertion)* leading to more appropriate labels :  

```
• "Hit Singles"         ──(+N)──>  "Hi[n]t Singles"
• "As Seen On TV"       ──(+V)──>  "As Se[v]en On TV"
• "Not Sold In Stores"  ──(+I)──>  "Not Sol[i]d In Stores"
• "Stereo LP"           ──(+A)──>  "Stereo [A]LP"
```

This establishes the central mechanic: **Every puzzle item undergoes a single-letter deletion into a proper name or word**.

<br/>

### Track-by-Track Decryption
Each entry on the tracklist of the album is a famous hit single with a little twist. Rather than modifying the song title, each clue targets the **recording artist's name**, altering it by inserting exactly one letter to fit the parodical title. 

For each entry we have to identify the original song and recording artist and find which letter to insert to create a new name fitting with the parodic title. For example :
- **Weezer** $+$ **H** $\rightarrow$ **Wheezer** *(out of breath after running a 5k)*
- **Bad Bunny** $+$ **L** $\rightarrow$ **Bald Bunny** (*"sin cabello"* = bald)
- **Luke Combs** $+$ **O** $\rightarrow$ **Luke Combos** *(cheddar pretzel snacks)*
- **State Champs** $+$ **U** $\rightarrow$ **Statue Champs** *(elevated onto a plinth)*

<br/>

Isolating the inserted letter from each track in sequential order yields the following key sentence : `HELP OUR DRUMMER IS OUT SICK`

## The secret artist

The orange price sticker **\$19.65** is a visual pun for the release year **1965**, where the \#1 hit matching the first word of our extracted message is **"Help!"** by **The Beatles**.

Then, the key sentence **"Help our drummer is out sick "** is another parodical title and we should find a way to insert a letter in The Beatles. A band without a drummer is beatless *(Beatles + s)*.

Finally, the secret track is from the **The Beatless**
